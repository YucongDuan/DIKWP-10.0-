# Source Material Synthesis

## Energy axis

The uploaded energy material frames energy as the driver of change and develops the link between energy, entropy, information processing and intelligence. In particular, the semantic compression and intention coupling discussion motivates the system's distinction between raw computation and meaningful information work. In this package, that becomes the `E` field and the field-coupling score.

## Information axis

The uploaded information material frames information as uncertainty reduction, a physical process with thermodynamic cost, and a bridge from data to purpose through DIKWP. In this package, that becomes the `I` field and the requirement that no generated object counts as semantic until it reduces uncertainty and preserves residual.

## Intent axis

The uploaded intent draft defines intention as a structure containing target, value, constraint, time and feedback. In this package, even intent is first negated. Local intent is later regenerated only as a reversible `P` field inside a DIKWP object.

## Synthesis

The final system does not claim that energy, information and intent have been unified by settled physics. It builds a research substrate in which energy-like gradients, information-like uncertainty shifts and intent-like feedback are jointly simulated, audited and returned to null.
